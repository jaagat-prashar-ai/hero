Motivation for Task 4 
- Paraphrase Problem: 
    - Rows 1 and 2 above are the same underlying claim - "steer around an obstruction on one side" - but worded completely differently (i.e,. "nudge right...clearance to pedestrians" vs "steer right... traffic cones")
    - A naive string/exact match on coc_text would treat every rollout as reasoning something unique, even when many are making the identical causal claim 
    - Since the whole point of "Same_reasoning" pairs is finding rollouts that do claim the same thing but differ in the resulting trajectory (or vice versa), you can't mine any pairs without first normalizing free text into a comparable structure
    # e.g., {action: nudge_right, cause: obstruction_lane_edge} - the same way Task 3 turned raw (T, 3) waypoints into a comparable maneuever_class label
