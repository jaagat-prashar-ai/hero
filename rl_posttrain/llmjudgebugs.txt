# Two code-side causes combine: 
1. _CheckpointUploader in rl_posttrain/training/run.py (line ~428) uploads every checkpoint file to S3 but never unlinks the local copy - it explicitly defers pruning to cosmos-rl's max_keep. 
2. max_keep = 10 in the TOML, so cosmos-rl won't prune anything until 10 checkpoints (600 GB) exist - the node dies at 3.
