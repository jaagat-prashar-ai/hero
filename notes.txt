# logit idea:

1. Pure logit analysis (token_alternative_map): During the existing generation pass (output_logits=True is already set), capture the top-k runner-up tokens at each
reasoning position - no re-running needed
2. Counterfactual sweep (Counterfactual_sweep): For selected positions (ranked by entropy or runner-up probability), inject a ForcedTokenAtStep logits processor that hard-forces an alternative token at step S, lets subsequent reasoning re-sample condiitoned on that choice, 
that runs the diffusion expert with a common seed to isolate trajectory impact.

- [rollout] batch_size = 2 prompts per rollout step; train_batch_per_replica = 48
